

This module allows users to see assets records linked to vendor bills on the purchase order itself.


**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:



Usage
=====

Guidelines for use:

 * Create an asset model and set Depreciation Account on it. *Accounting > Configuration > Management > Asset Models
 * Create new Purchase order and confirm it. If product bill policy is set to 'after receiving quantity' then confirm the transfer/receipt.
 * Go to Vendor bills and confirm it with appropriate Account.Assets records are visible on bills.
 * On Purchase Orders they are also visible and can be reached by clicking on the buttoon 'Assets'. 
 * To see the asset records . *Accounting > Accounting > Management > Assets
