How does the deployment process work?
  • ↳ How to purchase? Add the module to your cart, proceed with payment, and wait for Odoo to validate the transaction. You can then download the ZIP file directly from your invoice.
  • ↳ How to install? Deploy the module directly into your server's addons path following standard Odoo procedures. Do not use the "Upload Module" feature within the Odoo Apps UI, as it lacks the stability required for enterprise environments.
  • ↳ How to upgrade? Always delete the existing module folder completely before extracting the new version. Overwriting files can leave legacy code behind, which often leads to unpredictable "ghost" errors.
  • ↳ How to migrate? Migration refers to moving between Odoo core versions. If you need to migrate specific user workspace settings, please contact us for dedicated support. Otherwise, perform a standard installation on your new Odoo instance.
  • ↳ Can I return after purchase? Please review all requirements carefully before buying. Our product quality is solid and we fully support verified technical issues. For more details, see our Sales Conditions.
Can I modify the source code for my specific needs?

↳ You are free to extend the logic for your licensed environment. However, any direct modification to our core files immediately voids the 48-hour SLA. We guarantee the stability of our original architecture. Once you change the "engine," you become the architect of that change. We support our craftsmanship, not external experiments.

What if a third-party module breaks the layout?

↳ We follow Odoo's official standards to ensure maximum compatibility. However, we cannot be held responsible for third-party modules that violate Odoo's framework logic.

↳ If a conflict occurs, we will help identify the root cause. If the fix is minor and straightforward, we'll patch it for you immediately. For more complex issues originating from external code, we will provide clear guidance on the next steps or how to approach the respective vendor. We protect our architecture, but we never leave our partners stranded.

Can I use the same license for my staging servers?

↳ Yes. We respect the professional development lifecycle. A single license covers one Production Identity, but we fully permit additional non-production databases (Dev, Staging, Testing) dedicated to that specific project. We don't charge you for your safety nets; we charge for the value you deliver in production. One Production Environment, One License.

What defines a "Response" and "Resolution" under your 48-hour SLA?

↳ Our 48-hour SLA is a commitment to high-performance support, and it relies on a data-driven process. To ensure a swift and accurate resolution, we require Technical Evidence (Server logs and reproduction steps) to officially trigger the support window.

↳ This requirement eliminates guesswork and allows our team to focus directly on the solution from the very first minute. The 48-hour clock begins once the necessary facts are provided. We prioritize efficiency and system integrity; by providing clear documentation, you help us deliver the excellence your business deserves.

I purchased the module months ago but only just installed it, and it's failing. What now?

↳ A long gap between purchase and installation often leads to an Environment Mismatch due to infrastructure or Odoo updates in the meantime.

↳ To maintain the 48-hour SLA, we perform a License Integrity Check to confirm the module is deployed in the environment intended for this license. If there is a fingerprint mismatch, we re-verify project details before proceeding.

Can I use a single license for multiple client deployments?

↳ Standard licenses are strictly 1:1 per production environment. This ensures dedicated support and update integrity for every instance.
